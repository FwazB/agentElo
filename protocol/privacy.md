# Public Artifact Privacy Contract

Public receipts and cards are constructed from a narrow allowlist. They are never generated by redacting captured Computer History.

Allowed content is limited to:

- version constants and enums;
- opaque random player, competition, and match IDs;
- ISO week identifiers;
- Form score and aggregate confidence values;
- Elo ratings, deltas, K, counters, and deterministic calculation strings;
- public receipt fingerprints;
- fixed privacy booleans.

Forbidden content includes raw history, event text, messages, prompts, URLs, links, contacts, names, handles, email addresses, phone numbers, filenames, paths, repository/project names, application/window titles, notes, tags, arbitrary metadata, and private evidence or evidence excerpts.

The fixed SVG XML namespace and fixed local `url(#...)` paint references are
format boilerplate. No observed, user-provided, or network-resolving URI is
accepted in a card.

Required tests seed private evidence with canaries and assert those canaries never appear in JSON, SVG, or PNG-adjacent text. Unknown keys at every public object depth must fail validation. Changing private evidence while keeping approved aggregate inputs identical must produce byte-identical public receipts and SVG cards.
